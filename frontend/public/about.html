About AgriConnect

Our Mission

AgriConnect is dedicated to addressing food insecurity in South Sudan by connecting farmers, extension officers, investors, and communities through a comprehensive digital platform. We believe that by improving access to information, markets, and resources, we can empower South Sudan's agricultural sector to feed its growing population.

The Problem

South Sudan faces significant challenges in providing sufficient food to its rising population. Limited access to market information, agricultural resources, extension services, and investment opportunities hinder the growth of the agricultural sector. Many farmers, especially those in rural areas, lack the tools and knowledge to increase their productivity.

Our Solution

AgriConnect provides a unified platform where:

  • Extension Officers can upload learning resources, post announcements, and manage agricultural product listings in the marketplace
  • Farmers receive market trends, learning materials, and announcements relevant to their location
  • Cooperatives enable illiterate farmers to participate through group accounts managed by leaders
  • Investors and NGOs can post opportunities for funding, training, and grants
  • The Public can browse the marketplace, view opportunities, read success stories, and explore agricultural activities across the country

How It Works

The extension officer is the core figure in the system. Admin creates extension officer accounts based on location and specialization. Extension officers then serve as mediators, uploading resources for farmers, managing marketplace listings with product details (name, quantity, price in SSP, and photos), and posting announcements.

Farmers who can read and write have individual accounts to access all platform features. For illiterate farmers, cooperatives provide a single account managed by a leader, ensuring no one is left behind.

Coverage

AgriConnect covers all 10 states of South Sudan: Central Equatoria, Eastern Equatoria, Western Equatoria, Jonglei, Unity, Upper Nile, Lakes, Warrap, Northern Bahr el Ghazal, and Western Bahr el Ghazal.
